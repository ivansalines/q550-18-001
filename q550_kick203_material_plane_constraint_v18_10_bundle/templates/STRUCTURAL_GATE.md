# Independent physical-structure gate

v18.10 reports only what the pinned **actual support ODE** and archived full-11D trajectories support. A metric on the 11D state space is not physically privileged merely because it produces a smooth or reproducible angle.

For a subsequent *physical* certification, provide from model derivation with independent source provenance:

1. Explicit mapping from all 11 numerical states to physical variables and their dimensions/units.
2. Any genuine constraints C(y)=constant or C(y)=0 and their derivatives DC,D2C. Confirm DC f=0 and consistency of initial condition.
3. If available, a mechanical/constitutive quadratic form G(y) (and coordinate pushforward), independently justified by energy or a variational principle.
4. A constraint-preserving initial tangent u with DC u=0 and second variation v such that DC v + D2C[u,u]=0. The archived second-order off-constraint source does **not** satisfy this automatically.
5. For any candidate gauge or geometric body-plane identification, the observation map and its geometric assumptions and units.

Avoid interpreting a zero Jacobian row as an invariant without checking f_i, and avoid treating coordinate normalization thresholds as intrinsic rank certificates. GL covariance of exterior forms is algebraic; it does not certify physical torsional motion.
