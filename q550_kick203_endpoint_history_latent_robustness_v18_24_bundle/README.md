# Q550 v18.24 — Endpoint-Preserved History Scrambling & Latent Closure Robustness Audit

## Scientific question

Does the chronology of *past* reduced observations improve prediction of the
future beyond a fixed present observation? And does a sensor-only predictive
closure learned at xi=4 transfer to xi=3.5 and 4.5 when it has not been
retrained? Do validation-selected latent ranks 10 and 20 retain their apparent
utility on unseen perturbations and shifted reference sections?

**Important distinction:** this is a model-blind *predictor*, not a model-blind
experiment designer. All observations are generated by a pinned original Q550
ODE or, for the frozen-only preflight, by an authenticated archived tangent
propagator. The material3 projection was previously designed using ODE data.
The predictor itself is fed only reduced sampled observations and observed
future targets; it never sees full states, the ODE, Jacobian or propagator.

## Protocol and null controls

- Fixed diagnostic projections: `material3` (3 components), `raw4` (4),
  `raw6` (6) of the material 10D sector, phase kept equal within paired
  perturbation/reference trajectories. The 11th coordinate is the phase.
- Three temporal anchor sections `xi={3.5,4.0,4.5}`. Training and validation
  are **only** at `xi=4.0`, with 96 and 32 random perturbations. Test samples
  at xi=4.0 and the two shifted anchors use 36 random unseen directions,
  plus one special independently held-out hidden direction per sensor.
- Note that changing xi on the reference orbit changes phase **and** material
  posture. These are *section-transfer tests*, NOT independent pure-clock-
  phase interventions. Shifted hidden probes transport and renormalize the
  xi=4 seed; they are NOT newly certified as hidden at the shifted anchors.
- History: 17 distinct reduced samples at offsets [-0.5,0]. Future targets
  at [+0.2,+0.5,+1.0]; prediction is normalized by the known perturbation
  amplitude (but this normalization is performed by the data producer).
- Training and validation amplitude: 1e-5; independent test amplitude 3e-6.
- Add independent synthetic Gaussian noise **only** to observed histories:
  sigma = {0,1e-4,1e-3}, on normalized coordinates. True future targets
  remain clean. No claim is made about real measurement noise.
- The `endpoint` model sees only the last sample.
- `chronological` preserves all 17 sample times.
- `recent` sees the last four samples.
- `past_random_per_trial` shuffles the **first 16 samples independently in
  each train/validation/test trajectory**; the final (present) sample remains
  *bit-for-bit identical* to the chronological sample. Three independent
  permutation replicates are included. The unordered past values are retained.
  Results measure the effect on this linear ridge estimator, not the
  information-theoretic impossibility of predicting from an unordered set.
- `past_reversed_global` reverses the first 16 samples for every trajectory
  in the *same deterministic way*, leaving the final sample unchanged. This
  is an invertible global reindexing: the linear ridge prediction must remain
  unchanged. The audit FAILS if this invariance is violated.
- Every model's ridge penalty is selected using reference xi=4 validation
  targets ONLY. Test targets, including shifted sections, never select a fit.
- Latent SVD closure: observed histories alone determine the axes. Ranks
  [6,8,10,12,15,20] are **all** recorded, including forced 10 vs 20
  comparisons, and the rank selected on xi=4 validation. No latent rank is
  interpreted as a number of physical degrees of freedom.

## Original Q550 provenance and execution

The bundle embeds the **unaltered v18.23 original server sendback**, including
its v18.23.1 rank-tie hotfix and nested older sendbacks. At read time,
`checkpoint_io_v18_24.py` validates its CRC, root, every SHA256 manifest
entry and original nonlinear proof, then recursively loads/validates the
older Q550 checkpoints. The original RHS is loaded **only on the server** from
its pinned source files (SHA256 checked against reference dependencies).
The nonlinear server script independently integrates perturbed orbits against
reference trajectories at each anchor; it does not substitute the tangent
cocycle for nonlinear data. No 400-period reference orbit rebuild is needed.

`frozen_*` outputs are a mathematical preflight based on archived tangent
propagators. `original_*` outputs will exist only after original nonlinear
server execution. The resulting CSVs list all models, observers, sigma,
anchors, ridge choices, matched hidden probes, and rank-10/rank-20 comparisons.
The PNG shows held-out prediction RMSE with preserved endpoint and three
independent past permutations.

All Python scripts to generate the new audit outputs, the model-blind estimator,

manifest and ZIP. The ZIP has **one extraction root**.

## Interpretive limits

1. Predictive history dependence of a reduced coordinate is not proof of
   constitutive material memory beyond a deterministic higher-dimensional ODE.
2. Independent per-trial shuffles make the linear estimator's input harder to
   interpret; failures are not an intrinsic information-theoretic proof.
3. Section transfer is phase-plus-posture transfer, not a phase-only intervention.
4. Forced latent dimension 20 does not create 20 physical state dimensions.
5. The Q550 diagnostic perturbations are off-constraint unless a constraint
   manifold is explicitly certified; no invariant metric or physical torsional
   flip is asserted by this audit.
